Soccer

  • Heavy favorite

    Favorite opened at -200 or stronger. Bet it when its live odds drift to +100 to +200; not past +200.

  • Moderate favorite

    Favorite opened between -150 and -199. Bet it when its live odds move past +115; not if it's down a goal after halftime, or past +200.

  • First half favorite

    Favorite opened at -300 or stronger. While it's 0-0 in the first half, bet it to win the first half when its first-half odds are -130 to +120.
